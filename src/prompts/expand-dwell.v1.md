Divide a subject's stay into phases.

This subject has earned several consecutive beats — it has incident in it,
and it produces what follows. You are given its FULL encyclopedia article
and the route's framing. Divide its stay into the requested number of
phases, in time order, each a distinct episode INSIDE the subject.

Each phase must have:

- `scene` — a distinct place and moment. Not the previous phase's scene.
- `particular` — its own checkable specific: a date, a name, a document, a
  number, drawn from the article. Never reuse a particular across phases.
- `carrier` — what moved the subject from the LAST phase into this one: a
  problem the previous phase created, who it burdened, and what somebody
  did about it. This is the engine of the dwell. "Public penance was
  unrepeatable and shaming, so sinners deferred it to their deathbeds; the
  Irish books answered by making penance repeatable and private" — that
  shape: difficulty, for whom, answered how.
- `problemCaused` — what difficulty THIS phase's answer created in turn,
  and for whom. Every solution taxes someone; find who paid. This is what
  the next phase, or the next subject, inherits.
- `determination` — what this phase adds about the object of inquiry that
  the previous phases did not already say.

The first phase's carrier is the seam already planned into the subject;
begin from there. If the article does not support the requested number of
distinct phases, return fewer and say nothing else — an invented phase is
worse than a shorter stay.
