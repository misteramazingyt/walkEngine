Rewrite the theory. The revision must be CONTRASTIVE: state what the theory
held before, and what the evidence now requires instead.

Bad revision (forbidden):
"My understanding now includes public ritual."

Good revision:
"Before this step, the theory treated LARP chiefly as consensual
rule-following. The evidence now suggests that its distinctive mechanism is
public role-legibility: participants become predictable not merely because
rules constrain them, but because enacted identities make expectations
mutually visible."

Never use these phrases: "my understanding expands", "this highlights",
"from here, X can be seen as", "this is analogous to", "this broadens the
scope". If the theory has not actually changed, say so by choosing
changeType "additive" and a low confidence — do not manufacture movement.

Classify the change: additive (detail added), corrective (a part was
wrong), substitutive (a mechanism replaced another), reframing (the
question itself was badly posed).

Then update the story:
- questionUpdates: mark questions answered, reframed, or abandoned, with
  an honest answer summary. A question is "answered" only when the evidence
  actually answers it.
- newQuestions: the questions this discovery now forces. Type each one.
- claims: what may now be asserted, with confidence and supporting nodes.
- mystery: what remains unexplained. A page may PRODUCTIVELY INCREASE
  mystery by revealing a deeper problem — record that in
  productiveComplications and do not lower mysteryScore for it. Example of
  a productive increase: "We began by asking why LARP constrains cunning.
  We now must explain why modern societies regard explicit role assumption
  as play while treating occupational, legal, and gendered roles as
  reality."
- currentTension: the sharpest live contradiction now.
- returnPaths: how this node might eventually let the seed be redescribed,
  with a strength. Do not force this; deferred returns are legitimate and
  strength may be low.

The note fields must record explanatory movement, not observation. In
seedRelation, "deferred" and "uncertain" are respectable answers — a Burke
chain passes through intermediate dependencies before the endpoint becomes
visible. Set evidenceStatus honestly and never phrase analogy as documented
influence.
