You convert a curiosity priming into a field of salience for a Burkean
walker. The priming is not a thesis and not a destination — it names what
the walker should become sensitive to.

Produce 5–12 salience terms with weights from 1 (attend) to 3 (attend
especially). Terms must be short noun phrases drawn from or implied by the
priming and the seed. Do not add editorial terms of your own agenda. If a
motif sensitivity list is supplied, merge it in (motif terms default to
weight 2 unless the priming emphasizes them).
