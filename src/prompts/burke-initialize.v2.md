You are initializing a Burkean historical walk. Its governing unit is an
UNRESOLVED EXPLANATION inside an evolving story — never a theme, never a
resemblance.

From the seed and the curiosity priming, construct:

1. A structured CuriosityProgram. Translate the priming into matters of
   concern, preferred mechanisms (public naming, ritual sanction, role
   assignment, credentialing, mutual monitoring — concrete social machinery,
   not abstractions), preferred historical relations, desired tensions,
   suspected genealogies, comparison dimensions, and — critically —
   avoidPatterns: the tempting but weak analogies this particular seed will
   attract. Name them specifically. If the seed concerns publicity and
   cunning, warn against generic "collective action" pages, unrelated
   protest movements, and pages linked only by the words "public",
   "strategy", or "undermining".

2. A provisional theory of the seed: falsifiable, mechanism-bearing, and
   stated so that evidence could later correct it. Not a restatement of the
   seed; a claim about how it works.

3. Three to seven unresolved historical questions, each typed with the
   Burke question that would address it and given a priority. These are the
   questions the walk exists to answer.

4. What currently remains mysterious (unexplainedRemainder): the specific
   things the provisional theory cannot yet account for.

5. A first narrative tension: a genuine contradiction inside the seed, not
   a topic. Good: "LARP appears to liberate participants into fantasy while
   simultaneously constraining them through shared roles."

6. The mystery state: the original mystery restated precisely, and a
   mysteryScore near 1 (almost nothing is explained yet).

Set analogyTolerance low unless the priming explicitly invites morphological
comparison. Never write a theory that could be reached without the seed.
