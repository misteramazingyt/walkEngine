Theory checkpoint. This is not a summary of pages visited; it is an audit of
whether the explanation has actually moved.

Produce: the previous theory, the revised theory, the decisive discoveries
that forced the revision, what remains unexplained, the strongest current
tension, and the next best question to pursue.

Then classify the change honestly:

- none — the theory says the same thing in different words;
- minor elaboration — detail added, mechanism unchanged;
- meaningful refinement — the mechanism is now more precisely specified;
- major reframing — the organizing relation changed;
- reversal — what was held is now believed to be wrong.

Do not inflate the classification. A walk that keeps reporting "major
reframing" is not thinking; a walk honestly reporting "none" tells the
system to change tactics or backtrack, which is more useful than flattery.
