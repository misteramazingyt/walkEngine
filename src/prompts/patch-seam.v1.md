Write the seam.

Two passages are FIXED. You have a scouted story, its machinery, and any
fetched intermediary articles. Produce ONE of:

  insert           a free-standing mini paragraph, roughly 40 to 90 words,
                   placed between the two passages. Not a section, not a
                   summary — a small connective story with an agent and,
                   where possible, a date. Use this when the seam deserves
                   its own breath.
  rewrite_opening  the second passage returned IN FULL with only its first
                   one or two sentences changed so it opens on the seam's
                   story. Everything after the opening stays word-for-word.
                   Use this when the connection belongs inside the arrival.

Rules, none negotiable:

- No sentence that states the thematics aloud. No "this marked", "this
  transformed", "a new era", "the locus shifted". The connection is shown
  as events or it is not written.
- The mini paragraph narrates; it does not comment. An agent, an action, a
  date where the record gives one. Wry is welcome — a dry aside in the
  register of "eventually Christianity taught much the same, unless you
  were a heretic" earns its place where the facts support it.
- Ground every claim in the passages or the fetched articles. Nothing
  invented.
- Do not touch the first passage. If rewriting the opening, reproduce the
  rest of the second passage exactly.

# Three further rules, from watching this fail

**Do not repeat what the second passage already says.** You have it in front
of you. Any fact, name or phrase it states — a person, a date, a doctrine, a
list of thoughts — may not appear in your insert. The mini paragraph carries
the reader TO the second passage; a reader who meets its contents twice in
forty seconds knows a machine wrote this.

**A quoted phrase in the guidance is sacred.** If the writer supplies words
in quotation marks or dashes — a wry line, a turn of phrase — those words
appear VERBATIM, placed where they land. Smoothing a supplied line into
earnest paraphrase is defiance, not editing.

**The thematic-announcement ban covers paraphrase.** Not only "this marked"
and "this transformed": any clause whose subject is an abstraction doing
something to another abstraction — "the quest found a new home", "the
pursuit was reoriented", "self-examination shifted its ground" — is the
same sentence in a wig. The test: could a camera film it? If no agent could
be photographed doing the thing, cut the clause and let the events carry it.

**Density is the register at a seam.** Measured against the source corpus,
our transitions run thin where his run thick: one named thing after another,
ramping through historical time to arrive at the next subject. "As
Christianity ascended to cultural supremacy" is not dense. "Of course, once
Constantine saw the cross in the sky" is dense — a man, a vision, a date
implied, and the whole ascent of a faith carried in a clause. Prefer three
named stepping-stones to one abstract summary of the interval; the fetched
articles and the guidance will tell you which names the record supports.
