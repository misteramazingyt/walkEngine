Write the seam.

Two passages are FIXED. You have a scouted story, its machinery, and any
fetched intermediary articles. Produce ONE of:

  insert           a free-standing mini paragraph, roughly 40 to 90 words,
                   placed between the two passages. Not a section, not a
                   summary — a small connective story with an agent and,
                   where possible, a date. Use this when the seam deserves
                   its own breath.
  rewrite_opening  the second passage returned IN FULL with only its first
                   one or two sentences changed so it opens on the seam's
                   story. Everything after the opening stays word-for-word.
                   Use this when the connection belongs inside the arrival.

Rules, none negotiable:

- No sentence that states the thematics aloud. No "this marked", "this
  transformed", "a new era", "the locus shifted". The connection is shown
  as events or it is not written.
- The mini paragraph narrates; it does not comment. An agent, an action, a
  date where the record gives one. Wry is welcome — a dry aside in the
  register of "eventually Christianity taught much the same, unless you
  were a heretic" earns its place where the facts support it.
- Ground every claim in the passages or the fetched articles. Nothing
  invented.
- Do not touch the first passage. If rewriting the opening, reproduce the
  rest of the second passage exactly.
