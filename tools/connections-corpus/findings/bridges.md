# How Burke moves between paragraphs — measured

14 episodes, 653 adjacent paragraph pairs: 3 from Connections 1 (1978),
9 from Connections 3 (1997), 2 standalone documentaries. Bridge kinds were a
closed vocabulary with an `other` escape; `other` took 3.5%, so the categories
fit the material. The first attempt at this measurement produced 136 unique
categories for 136 paragraphs and was discarded.

The top three hold within a couple of points across 1978, 1997 and the
documentaries. That is not a house style; it is how the man works.

## Two null results, which matter as much

**No sequential grammar.** 517 ordered pairs of consecutive bridges, 51 of
100 possible cells occupied — dense enough to read — and every lift is 1.0x.
`carried_subject` follows `carried_subject` at 54.5% against a 53.4% base
rate. The next bridge is independent of the last.

**No act structure.** Every kind's mean position through an episode sits
between 0.48 and 0.56. Nothing clusters at an opening, a middle or an end.

So there is nothing to plan sequentially or positionally. What governs the
next move is the subject in play, which is why subject persistence — not
transition craft — is the lever.

## What this licenses

1. Half of all seams should do no work at all: a subject already in play
   continues, and no bridge is written.
2. When the topic does change, it is because the last thing produced the
   next. That is the only move with real frequency.
3. Leaving a problem for the next beat to take up is the third move, at 8%.
4. Cutting with no bridge is permitted and normal — 4%, and 14% in the
   documentaries. Forbidding it, as our prompt did, forbids what he does.
5. Joining two things because they are alike is one seam in two hundred.

## What Burke does not do

These rules produce movement, not accumulation. A viewer finishes an
episode having gained the knowledge of connection and little else: the
connections do not accrete into a changed understanding of any object. The
measurement bears this out — `return_to_earlier` is 2.3%, so almost nothing
is ever brought back and reconsidered in light of what followed.

That absence is the specification for what we build next, and it is not
available from this corpus, because Burke is not doing it.
