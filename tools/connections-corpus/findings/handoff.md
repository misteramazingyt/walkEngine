# The machinery under a topic change

166 topic-change seams across 14 episodes, classified by what makes the next
subject's arrival earned — the machinery, not the phrasing.

| mechanism | share |
| --- | --- |
| changed_conditions | 26.5% |
| pure_sequence | 23.5% |
| created_demand | 16.9% |
| object_travels | 12.0% |
| person_travels | 12.0% |
| parallel_joined | 5.4% |
| other | 3.6% |

Personal texture — a homely, anecdotal register: same shop, notes passed,
met again years later — rides on 30.7% of seams, across mechanisms.

Read together: the environmental loop (output changes the shared world; the
changed world summons or feeds the next subject) is the commonest machinery
at 43.4% combined, and is therefore the meta-bridge worth inscribing. But a
quarter of seams have no machinery at all — the narration simply moves — so
the loop must never be forced onto every seam; an honest cut is a quarter of
the practice. And the quaint personal seam is less a mechanism than a
register that a third of all seams wear.
